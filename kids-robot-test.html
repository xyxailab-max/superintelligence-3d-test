How does this work?

Robo can't really "see" like you do. It only notices simple things, like colour, shape and size. When it meets a new picture, it looks for the picture it remembers that is most like it, and guesses the same answer. That's how a lot of real AI learns: from lots of examples! When Robo is wrong and you tell it, it learns from its mistake.
