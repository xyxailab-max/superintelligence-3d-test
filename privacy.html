Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Short version: we only collect what you type into our forms. No ads, no tracking cookies, no selling your data.

Who we are

Use Superintelligence (usesuperintelligence.com) is run by Akmal Ibragimov. Contact: hello@usesuperintelligence.com.

What we collect

  • Creator listing form: your name or brand, email, product name, type, price, product link, description, image and (optional) social profile.
  • Emails you send us: your address and what you write.
  • Basic server logs (IP address, browser, pages visited) kept by our hosting provider for security.

How we use it

To review and publish your listing, reply to you, and keep the site secure. Your email stays private and is never shown on the site. We don't sell or rent personal data.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, so there's no cookie banner. Our pages load fonts and code libraries from Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs (Cloudflare), which receive your IP address to deliver those files.

Third parties

Hosting and DNS (Cloudflare, GitHub), email (Google Workspace), and the services above. Links to creator products and partner tools take you to their sites, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Listing details are kept while your listing is live or under review. Emails are kept as long as needed to help you.

Your rights & deleting your data

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Email hello@usesuperintelligence.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and we'll handle it within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights under local law.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13 (16 in the EU), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.
