How does this work?

A computer can't guess what you mean. It only does exactly what you tell it, one step at a time, in the right order. A list of steps like this is called a program, and writing one is called coding. If Robo bumps into a rock, the steps have a mistake (a "bug"). Fixing it is called debugging!
